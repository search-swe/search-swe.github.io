01 · Motivation · Why

Building search is an engineering problem, not a single-model choice.

01

Real systems have many parts

Reliable search requires data inspection, indexing, retrieval, filtering, reranking, interfaces, and validation.

02

Expert choices must be engineered

Agents must turn retrieval knowledge into robust choices about models, tools, data flow, and system constraints.

03

Working systems are the evidence

A plausible plan is not enough. The final artifact must build, run, and produce useful search results.

? THE QUESTION

Can a coding agent build, optimize, and repair a real search system?

02 · Search-SWE · What & How

Real search problems in. Working systems out.

Agents implement, optimize, or repair a search system under fixed constraints. Search-SWE rebuilds each submission and evaluates what actually runs.

⌁

Key findings

  • Agents are markedly stronger at locally verifiable search repairs than at open-ended search-system design.
  • Agents can use and combine different search components, but often struggle to understand the task's core objective.
  • Agents struggle to optimize search quality, latency, memory, and engineering completeness simultaneously.
  • Public test sets strongly steer agent behavior and readily induce heuristic overfitting.
  • Major gains often come from discovering the structure of the data, not from improving a general-purpose search algorithm.
